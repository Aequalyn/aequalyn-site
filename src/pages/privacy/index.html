Privacy policy

Your privacy

Last updated {{updated_legal}}.

This site is run by {{operator}} ("we"). It has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking across sites. This page says what little it does keep, why, and what you can do about it. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

What we keep

  • Counters. When you open an item's page, give it a heart or copy its install command, the counter for that item goes up. To count each visitor once a day and keep your heart yours, your browser gets a cookie named aev holding a random number. We store only a salted hash of that number, never your IP address. Daily view and copy records are deleted the next day; your hearts stay until you take them back.
  • Your choices on this site. The theme you wear, the view you prefer and the items you hearted are kept in your own browser (local storage). They never leave it, and clearing your browser's site data removes them.
  • Messages. If you write to us with the contact form, we receive your name, email address and message by email, and use them only to answer you. We delete them when the conversation is over, at the latest after one year.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider keeps standard web server logs (IP address, time, page, browser) for security and to keep the service running, for a limited period under its own policy.

Why, and on what basis

The counters and the cookie serve the site you asked for: showing how much an item is liked and used, and keeping your heart. That is our legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), and the cookie is needed for exactly that and nothing else. Answering a message rests on your request (Article 6(1)(b)). We sell nothing and share nothing with anyone.

Other services this site uses

Item pictures and the team's profile pictures are loaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, avatars.githubusercontent.com) and jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net), where they live. Like any server, theirs see your IP address when your browser fetches a picture. Links to GitHub take you to GitHub, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete what we hold about you, object to its use, or ask for a copy. Because the counters store only an anonymous hash, we usually cannot link them to you; delete the aev cookie and your browser starts over. Write to us through the contact page. You may also complain to your data protection authority; in Belgium that is the Gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit / Autorité de protection des données.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page changes with it, with a new date at the top. The history of every change is public in this site's repository.
