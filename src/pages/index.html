A desktop that wears your wallpaper.

Aequalyn is a Linux Distribution based on Arch Linux packaged into an ISO and shipped with the whole suite and desktop: a shaped bar and up to sixteen docks, a control center, a Store and an Atelier of themes, layouts and wallpapers, coloured across every app by the picture behind them.

Colours from the wallpaper

matugen reads the picture and recolours the shell, GTK, Qt, terminals and browsers. Or pin a folder theme and keep its palette.

{{icon:layout}}

Thirty-seven window layouts

Portrait, Centerpiece, Bento, Galleries, Diptychs and more, a floating desktop with snap zones, and a picker for all of them.

{{icon:store}}

A Store you can trust

Every Market item is checked by a bot and approved by a person at one pinned commit. Updates are reviewed the same way.

{{icon:bar}}

A bar shaped your way

Twenty-six looks for the bar, sixteen docks, three regions of plugins you drag where you want them.

{{icon:lock}}

Lock screens and login

A Quickshell lock with face unlock, and SDDM themes to match, drawn in the colours you wear.

{{icon:install}}

Install in minutes

A guided installer with eight profiles, an offline repository on the ISO, and updates that migrate your settings instead of replacing them.
